# Widget umbrella specification — Sleep Debt

Status: research-gated contract  
Umbrella ID: `rest.sleep_debt`

Question: **How much eligible sleep does the selected need model say remains
unmet across its declared window?**

Essential `1×1`: debt result, model/source, window and confidence/eligibility.

Provider debt remains provider-owned. A Body result cannot ship until sleep
need, adaptation, naps, missing nights and accumulation/repayment rules are
validated and versioned.

Variants and sizes remain pending that method decision. No universal need or
false precision.
