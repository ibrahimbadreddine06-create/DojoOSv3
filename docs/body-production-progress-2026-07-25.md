## Method-gated umbrellas

Training Load and Sleep Debt are present but do not manufacture a result. They
remain in an honest unavailable state until an eligible provider-owned result
or a separately validated and versioned Body method is available.

## External launch blockers

- Apply and verify canonical and operational migrations against the production database with backup and rollback.
- Complete provider authorization/partnership work and real OAuth/native-host integrations. The app currently reports these honestly as pending rather than simulating connectivity.
- Select and configure production secret encryption/KMS.
- Run device-level acceptance tests against native HealthKit/Health Connect hosts and production consent states.
- Complete privacy, consent, retention and jurisdictional review.
- Validate calculation protocols and sensitive health interpretations before making production claims.

## What the percentage means

100% measures only selected umbrella implementation coverage. It does not
measure global product completion, provider connectivity, scientific
validation, legal readiness or launch readiness.
