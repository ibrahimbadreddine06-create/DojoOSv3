# Widget umbrella specification — Heart Rate

Status: definitive observation contract  
Umbrella ID: `hub.heart_rate`

Question: **What heart rate was recorded in the selected context/period?**

Essential `1×1`: current/latest or period summary, bpm, source and coverage.

Variants can be all-day/current, activity-context or overnight-context while
retaining one coherent HR record contract. Resting Heart Rate remains separate.

Initial sizes: `1×1`, `1×2`, `2×1`, `2×2`. No medical rhythm interpretation
from ordinary PPG.
