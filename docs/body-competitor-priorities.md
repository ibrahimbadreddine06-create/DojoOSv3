# Body Competitor Research Priorities

Priority determines research depth, not product quality. The pool remains open
and a lower-priority product can be promoted when it exposes unique evidence.

## Research depth

### Tier A — full product reconstruction

Reconstruct the relevant product surface as completely as evidence permits:
metrics, inputs, calculations, states, visualizations, history, explanations,
device dependencies, manual paths, and limitations.

### Tier B — targeted deep dive

Investigate the product deeply for the specific capability in which it is
distinctive. Do not spend equal effort reconstructing unrelated surfaces.

### Tier C — gap and pattern scan

Use to test whether Tier A and B missed a meaningful metric, interaction,
visualization, niche user need, or regional pattern.

## Promotion rules

- Promote a product when it contains a capability or evidence category absent
  from the current higher tiers.
- Demote a product when its relevant behavior cannot be verified and it adds no
  unique perspective.
- Market popularity alone is insufficient; a technically distinctive niche
  product may deserve deeper research.
- Similar ownership does not imply identical product behavior. Connected
  products are still inspected separately where their surfaces differ.
- Research does not stop when every named product has been inspected. It stops
  only when new sources cease producing meaningful new product information.
