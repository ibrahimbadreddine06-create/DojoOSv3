# Widget umbrella specification — Products

Status: definitive logged-observation contract  
Umbrella ID: `hygiene.products`

## Operational contract

Assigning a product to a routine and recording use/notes are integral actions.
An assignment, planned use, actual use and user observation remain distinct.

Question: **Which configured products am I using, due to use, or evaluating?**

Essential `1×1`: current/next product, routine link, usage state and relevant
expiry/reaction context.

Data: product identity, optional reliable ingredients, opened/expiry date,
usage events and user observations.

Variants: due/current, routine set, usage/reaction history. Initial sizes:
`1×1`, `1×2`, `2×1`, `2×2`. Tracking never proves efficacy or safety.
