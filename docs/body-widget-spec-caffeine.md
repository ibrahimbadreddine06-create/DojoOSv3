# Widget umbrella specification — Caffeine

Status: optional information contract, variants pending  
Umbrella ID: `nutrition.caffeine`

## Operational contract

Quick record/edit is integral and writes an intake event with amount, time,
source and confidence. A configured plan remains separate until reconciled.

Question: **How much caffeine was logged, and when?**

Essential `1×1`: amount, most relevant timing context and completeness.

Inputs may be direct entries or derived from foods/beverages with confidence.
Timing remains central; unknown caffeine content is not zero.

Variants: total/timing, decay illustration only under a validated named model,
and period pattern. Initial sizes: `1×1`, `1×2`, `2×1`, `2×2`.

No causal sleep claim or universal safe cutoff. Detail exposes entries,
estimates, timing and history.
