# Widget umbrella specification — Resting Heart Rate

Status: definitive observation contract  
Umbrella ID: `rest.resting_heart_rate`

Question: **How does resting heart rate compare with my same-source baseline?**

Essential `1×1`: bpm, measurement context, source and baseline/state.

Use provider RHR or a standardized eligible resting sample; do not substitute
nightly-low HR without preserving that different meaning.

Variants: value/baseline, nightly trend, range. Initial sizes:
`1×1`, `1×2`, `2×1`, `2×2`. Deviations never establish their cause.
