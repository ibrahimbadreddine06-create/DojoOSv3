# Widget umbrella specification — Recovery

Status: conditional source-owned contract  
Umbrella ID: `rest.recovery`

Question: **What does my selected recovery source report, and what contributed?**

Essential `1×1`: result, provider/manual-method identity, freshness and concise
state. Oura, WHOOP, Garmin, Polar and other scores remain non-interchangeable.

Variants may expose provider score/contributors, component observations, or an
explicit subjective check-in. They never average unlike scores or imitate
wearable data.

Initial sizes: `1×1`, `1×2`, `2×1`, `2×2`. Detail exposes contributors,
baseline/window, source, missingness and history.
