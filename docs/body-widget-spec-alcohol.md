# Widget umbrella specification — Alcohol

Status: opt-in sensitive information contract  
Umbrella ID: `nutrition.alcohol`

## Operational contract

Opt-in quick record/edit is integral. It preserves privacy class, original
serving and manual provenance; planned and consumed states never collapse.

Question: **What alcohol amount and timing did I choose to log?**

Essential `1×1`: amount/frequency for the period, unit definition, privacy and
completeness state.

Store original beverage/serving and normalized standard amount only under an
explicit regional definition.

Variants: recent intake, timing pattern, frequency trend. Initial sizes:
`1×1`, `1×2`, `2×1`, `2×2`.

No moral score, hidden inference or causal recovery/sleep conclusion. Detail
exposes entries, unit method, privacy controls and history.
