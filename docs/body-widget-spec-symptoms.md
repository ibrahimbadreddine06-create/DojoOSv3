# Widget umbrella specification — Symptoms

Status: user-defined sensitive observation contract  
Umbrella ID: `hygiene.symptoms`

## Operational contract

Capture/edit is integral and supports user-defined typed scales/states without
enumerating the limit. A reminder or goal may link to the observation contract,
but cannot manufacture the observation itself.

Question: **How has one selected symptom/condition observation changed?**

Essential `1×1`: selected observation, latest value/state, scale and recency.
Each installed instance focuses on what the user chose; the product does not
enumerate the limit.

Variants: latest/baseline, trend, occurrence field. Initial sizes:
`1×1`, `1×2`, `2×1`, `2×2`.

No diagnosis or causal product/routine conclusion. Detail exposes scale,
context, privacy and history.
