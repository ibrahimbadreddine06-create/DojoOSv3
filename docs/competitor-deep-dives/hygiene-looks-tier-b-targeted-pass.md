# Hygiene & Looks Tier B — targeted evidence pass

Status: broad Tier B pass complete; some products need stronger direct capture  
Updated: 2026-07-25

## Evidence boundary

This domain mixes regulated reproductive products, general cycle trackers,
routine tools, image journals and treatment services. Their claims and risk
levels are not interchangeable. This pass records current first-party behavior
and leaves weak evidence explicitly weak.

## Cycle, fertility and reproductive-health products

### Natural Cycles

- A regulated use case converts temperature, period history and optional LH
  tests into a daily fertility status.
- It defaults conservatively when information is insufficient.
- Basal oral temperature and wearable distal skin temperature are not mixed
  across a device change.
- Predictions are explicitly changeable; current daily status has different
  meaning from a future prediction.
- Product mode and country determine whether it is offered as contraception or
  fertility monitoring.

### Spot On

- Joins period/cycle observations with contraception schedules and reminders.
- Different methods create different schedules, missed-dose guidance and
  fertility implications.
- Mood, activity and bodily observations can be logged alongside the cycle.
- Current public material says data is stored on-device/cloud backup rather
  than in a Spot On account.

### Stardust

- Current official material presents period, symptom, hormone-phase and
  pregnancy tracking with explanatory phase context.
- Its differentiating presentation layer uses cycle-phase storytelling.
- Public technical evidence is presently too thin for calculation claims.

### Glow

- Combines cycle length, BBT, LH tests and logged symptoms for fertility
  forecasting.
- Supports test-strip capture, charts, partner sharing and transitions into
  pregnancy tracking.
- It explicitly says the tracker is not reliable standalone contraception.

### Ovia

- Product modes cover cycle tracking, trying to conceive, pregnancy,
  postpartum and peri/menopause.
- The relevant distinction is longitudinal mode transition rather than a
  single perpetual dashboard.
- Current first-party public detail is insufficient for algorithm claims.

Product distinction: observation, retrospective phase, future prediction,
daily fertility status, contraception workflow and pregnancy state must remain
separate semantic facts.

## Skin, appearance and routine products

### FeelinMySkin

- Builds customizable morning/afternoon/evening routines from products and
  occasional actions.
- Tracks adherence, product usage, opening/expiration, cost, irritation and
  ingredients.
- A photo/lifestyle journal supports comparison over time without claiming
  causation.

### Miiskin

- Standardized close-up, face and full-body photography supports longitudinal
  comparison.
- Blurriness checks and guided capture improve input quality.
- Mole sizing uses a known-size reference object; the user remains responsible
  for the measured dimension.
- The consumer tool documents changes and supports provider sharing; it
  explicitly does not diagnose skin cancer.

### MDacne

- A selfie/questionnaire feeds personalized product recommendations.
- The selfie tracker records treatment progress and can support treatment
  adjustment.
- Public product claims need independent clinical and bias validation before
  informing Body.

### Finch

- User-defined self-care goals, reflections, breathing, timers and other
  activities are connected to gentle progress and rewards.
- The system deliberately allows custom goals and frames streaks as gentle
  consistency rather than perfection.
- This is adherence/motivation evidence, not physiological measurement.

## Cross-product evidence dimensions

- recurring routine versus one-off action;
- adherence versus biological outcome;
- subjective observation versus sensor measurement;
- standardized image capture versus image interpretation;
- retrospective fact versus prediction;
- wellness guidance versus regulated medical-device output;
- current state versus a longitudinal transition between life stages.

Everything may later be expressed inside an element slot. These dimensions are
**not** a proposed umbrella list.

## Remaining evidence tasks

- Direct current captures for all nine products.
- Stronger official technical reconstruction for Stardust and Ovia.
- Regulatory, regional, age and intended-use matrix for reproductive products.
- Independent validation and bias analysis for image-based systems.
- Privacy/threat-model review for cycle data and body imagery.
- Custom-routine semantics and outcome attribution research.
