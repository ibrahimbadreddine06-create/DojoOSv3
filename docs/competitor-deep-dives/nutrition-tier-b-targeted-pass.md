# Nutrition Tier B — targeted evidence pass

Status: partial Tier B pass; Noom still requires a better product reconstruction  
Updated: 2026-07-25

## Evidence boundary

This document is a product-pattern scan from current first-party sources. It
does not validate efficacy claims, does not reproduce proprietary coaching
algorithms and does not define Body widget umbrellas.

## MyNetDiary

- Customizable dashboard modes emphasize calories, macros, diabetes/blood
  glucose or low-carb use cases.
- Daily and weekly analysis combines intake, weight projection, nutrient
  highlights and recommendations.
- Food logging supports search, barcode and meal scanning; wearable data can
  contribute exercise and steps.
- The product claims a staff-verified database with up to 108 nutrients, but
  database quality still requires independent sampling.

Distinctive lesson: a single intake surface can be reconfigured around the
user's monitoring purpose without changing the underlying food facts.

## Carbon Diet Coach

- A weekly check-in reviews tracking completeness, average intake, macros,
  weight trend, goal and actual progress before changing targets.
- The Calorie Planner redistributes a weekly budget across unlocked days.
- “Macro Calories” derives energy from protein/carbohydrate/fat grams and
  deliberately differs from rounded label calories.
- Trend weight is used to dampen daily weight noise; exact algorithm remains
  undisclosed.
- Current coaching reports explain why targets changed or stayed unchanged.

Distinctive lesson: coaching output must expose the decision inputs, data
completeness and reason for change; it is not merely another progress metric.

## RP Diet Coach

- Current Day Balance can redistribute calories and macros across remaining
  meals after an unplanned intake.
- Eating windows express when meals can occur.
- Current product direction foregrounds calories and protein while treating
  carbohydrate/fat distribution as flexible.

Distinctive lesson: live replanning is an action workflow, separate from the
widget that summarizes intake.

## FoodNoms

- Tracks calories/macros plus selectable nutrients, water, caffeine, alcohol
  and other dietary facts.
- Advanced analysis includes calorie balance, charts for any tracked nutrient,
  top-food analysis and weekly/monthly history.
- It supports custom meals/recipes, one-day exceptions and Apple Health
  import/export.

Distinctive lesson: a nutrient is not automatically an umbrella; user-pinned
facts and deeper history can be different surfaces over the same intake data.

## ZOE

- The current consumer app emphasizes meal photo capture, meal scores, food
  swaps, plant diversity, processed-food risk and streaks rather than calorie
  counting.
- Vendor disclosure says meal scoring considers predicted blood-sugar/fat
  impact, macro balance and fiber, but exact weights are not public.

Distinctive lesson: food quality and behavior-change products form a separate
evidence family from energy/macro accounting.

## WaterMinder

- Hydration combines goal progress, quick logging, reminders, history and
  interactive widgets.
- Inputs may include different drink types; the product also exposes caffeine
  tracking and weather-adjusted goals.
- Widget forms include compact progress, remaining intake, character/body fill
  and direct log actions.

Distinctive lesson: hydration has both a state visualization and a frequent
action surface; the two must remain connected but need not be the same widget.

## Cross-product evidence produced

The Tier B scan reveals six distinct product questions that later synthesis
must preserve without prematurely grouping them:

- factual intake accounting;
- energy and macro balance;
- micronutrient/compound sufficiency;
- adaptive target coaching;
- food quality and dietary diversity;
- rapid logging and adherence.

These are research dimensions, **not** a proposed umbrella list.

## Remaining Tier B work

- Reconstruct Noom from current product/help material rather than a corporate
  overview.
- Obtain current direct captures for every product.
- Verify target algorithms and medical-condition features independently.
- Audit regional food-database coverage and manual fallback behavior.
