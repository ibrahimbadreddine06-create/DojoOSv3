# Activity Tier C — gap scan

Status: first-party pattern scan complete; direct app capture and scientific
validation remain  
Updated: 2026-07-25

## Evidence boundary

This is a deliberately concise scan of what each product adds to the Activity
research space. It records product patterns, data and presentation choices. It
does **not** define Body widget umbrellas, endorse vendor claims or treat a
proprietary score as scientifically validated.

## StrongLifts

- **Distinctive pattern:** turns a narrow strength method into an
  instruction-to-execution loop: prescribed exercise/set/rep/load, one-tap set
  completion, automatic rest, load progression and deload.
- **Data and views:** exercise load, reps, total volume, estimated 1RM,
  powerlifting total, body weight, PRs and calendar-like consistency. Its
  progress graphs keep exercise history inspectable rather than collapsing it
  into one readiness score.
- **Body relevance:** a completed set is simultaneously a logged fact and an
  input to the next prescription; those must remain distinguishable.
- **Confidence:** high for named functionality and metrics; formula/version
  audit is still required for e1RM and progression decisions.
- Sources: https://stronglifts.com/app/ and
  https://support.stronglifts.com/article/89-graphs

## Nike Training Club

- **Distinctive pattern:** coaching/content product rather than a dense
  performance dashboard. It combines follow-along video, clear
  set/rep “whiteboard” workouts, single sessions and phased multi-week
  programmes.
- **Data and views:** duration, modality, target area, equipment, schedule,
  programme progress, trophies and milestones are more central than advanced
  physiological analytics.
- **Body relevance:** instruction, planned work, completion and achievement
  are separate useful states even when no rich sensor stream exists.
- **Confidence:** high for workflow; low for current detailed progress
  visualization because Nike’s public pages expose little of the live UI.
- Sources: https://www.nike.com/help/a/ntc-info and
  https://www.nike.com/ntc-app

## adidas Running

- **Distinctive pattern:** GPS activity capture plus adaptive guided running,
  challenges, leaderboards and brand rewards.
- **Data and views:** distance, duration, pace, splits, elevation, calories,
  heart rate and cadence; voice coaching and training-plan feedback connect
  live execution to an adapting plan.
- **Body relevance:** the same recorded activity supports immediate
  performance, longitudinal progress, social competition and rewards without
  those becoming the same concept.
- **Confidence:** high for supported data and top-level flows; direct capture
  remains necessary for charts, history aggregation and plan-adaptation detail.
- Sources: https://www.adidas.com/us/running-app and
  https://www.adidas.com/us/help/us-adidas-runtastic/how-do-i-get-support-for-the-adidas-running-app

## MapMyRun

- **Distinctive pattern:** explicitly separates a workout from a route. A
  workout has activity type and duration; GPS/sensors enrich it, while a route
  is the spatial path that may be planned or generated by a recording.
- **Data and views:** map/elevation, duration, distance, pace, calories, splits,
  weather and gear; compatible sources add cadence, stride length, heart rate,
  power and speed. Time-series bars and graphs support split comparison;
  heart-rate zones and form coaching add interpretation.
- **Body relevance:** source capability and provenance determine which facts
  exist. Missing GPS cannot silently manufacture route, elevation or cadence.
- **Confidence:** high; the official support documentation is unusually
  explicit. Coaching logic and paid/free differences still need version audit.
- Sources:
  https://support.mapmyfitness.com/hc/en-us/articles/1500009133781-Difference-Between-Workouts-Routes,
  https://support.mapmyfitness.com/hc/en-us/articles/1500009117902-Viewing-a-Workout,
  and https://support.mapmyfitness.com/hc/en-us/articles/1500009118142-Garmin-Connect

## ROUVY

- **Distinctive pattern:** structured indoor cycling is shown as a sequence of
  target segments over a real-world virtual route. Planned target and actual
  effort appear together, while an execution score reports adherence.
- **Data and views:** duration, power targets, heart-rate zone, cadence, speed,
  FTP, TSS, segment profile and star execution. Separate insight cards cover a
  six-week 0–100 Training Score, Recovery, FTP progression, activity history,
  weekly distance/elevation/calories/duration and ride frequency.
- **Body relevance:** prescribed target, actual trace, execution quality,
  training load and recovery are different layers and should retain their
  provenance and time window.
- **Confidence:** high for current UI semantics; ROUVY labels Training Insights
  beta and does not publicly expose enough formula detail to validate its two
  proprietary 0–100 scores.
- Sources:
  https://support.rouvy.com/hc/en-us/articles/33398875835921-Workouts-in-ROUVY
  and
  https://support.rouvy.com/hc/en-us/articles/38691004407569-Training-Insights-in-the-Companion-App

## Freeletics

- **Distinctive pattern:** adaptive coaching uses goal, fitness level,
  equipment, available time, soreness and post-session difficulty feedback to
  select the next workout.
- **Data and views:** weekly plan, workout duration/structure and benchmark
  completion time; “God workouts” turn repeatable sessions into comparable
  performance tests.
- **Body relevance:** subjective feedback can legitimately alter future
  prescription, but it must not be presented as sensor-measured physiology.
- **Confidence:** medium-high for workflow; low for exact adaptation algorithm,
  progress visualizations and claims about its AI.
- Sources: https://www.freeletics.com/en/index.html and
  https://www.freeletics.com/en/hiit-workout-app/

## Centr

- **Distinctive pattern:** one product separates a personalized daily plan,
  structured programmes, an open content library and history/statistics. It
  also separates trainer-paced video workouts from self-guided, loggable sets.
- **Data and views:** schedule, completion history, programme progress, logged
  weight/reps/timed exercises, workout filters and Apple Watch workout data.
- **Body relevance:** content discovery, prescription, live guidance and
  performance logging are related but different workflows.
- **Confidence:** high for information architecture and logging features;
  limited public evidence for quantitative progress visuals.
- Sources:
  https://help.centr.com/en-US/how-do-i-navigate-the-centr-app-3233567,
  https://help.centr.com/en-US/how-do-i-browse-available-workouts-3233629,
  and https://help.centr.com/en-US/articles/workouts-and-programs-345223

## Ladder

- **Distinctive pattern:** a coach/team identity anchors a new weekly strength
  plan. Live execution combines video demonstrations, in-ear coaching, precise
  pacing and the user’s own music.
- **Data and views:** daily/weekly schedule, reps, weights, PR alerts, notes,
  workout consistency and Apple Watch heart rate/calories; progress is framed
  as adherence to progressive coach-authored programming.
- **Body relevance:** the useful unit is not only an isolated workout but the
  relationship among coach, programme week, session execution and logged
  progression.
- **Confidence:** high for product workflow; medium for exact chart semantics
  and progression rules.
- Sources: https://www.joinladder.com/ and
  https://www.joinladder.com/team/endure

## Sweat

- **Distinctive pattern:** programme-led training with an optional on-demand
  library, schedule/planner and a progress area; it supports home/gym contexts,
  equipment substitutions and many modalities.
- **Data and views:** monthly planned-versus-completed calendar, linked steps,
  target muscle groups, logged weights, programme progression and private
  progress photos.
- **Body relevance:** qualitative visual progress, adherence history and
  strength logs are distinct evidence types and should not be forced into one
  score.
- **Confidence:** high for workflow and named records; low for detailed live
  visualization and programme progression logic.
- Sources: https://sweat.com/training,
  https://support.sweat.com/hc/en-us/articles/360001610515-How-do-I-navigate-the-Sweat-app,
  and
  https://support.sweat.com/hc/en-us/articles/115007116808-How-do-I-save-and-share-my-progress-photos

## Seven

- **Distinctive pattern:** compresses exercise into a minimum viable daily
  habit, then uses challenges, lives/pause days, streaks, achievements and
  peer leagues to sustain repetition.
- **Data and views:** workout/circuit duration, completed workouts, daily
  calendar state, streak/challenge state, league rank and achievements.
  External workouts can count via manual logging or Apple Health.
- **Body relevance:** adherence can be the primary product outcome even when
  physiological depth is low; manually entered and imported completion need
  explicit provenance.
- **Confidence:** high for habit mechanics; scientific efficacy claims and the
  suitability of daily high-intensity work require independent review.
- Sources: https://seven.app/,
  https://seven.app/articles/explore-seven/what-is-the-7-month-challenge,
  and https://seven.app/articles/explore-seven/what-are-leagues

## Cross-product gaps exposed

- Strength products range from deterministic progression to opaque adaptive
  coaching; “personalized” is not one reproducible calculation.
- Planned demand, live execution, completed result and next prescription are
  separate facts.
- Route and workout are separate even when one recording creates both.
- Objective sensor data, user feedback, manual logs, photos and completion
  status require source/provenance labels.
- Progress can mean performance, load, skill, programme advancement,
  consistency or habit; research must preserve those meanings.
- A visualization is not evidence that the underlying score is validated.

These are research dimensions only. They are **not** proposed widget umbrellas.

## Remaining research gate

- Current direct captures at representative phone and desktop sizes.
- Exact formulas, windows, missing-data behavior and versioning for proprietary
  scores and adaptive prescriptions.
- Independent evidence review for effectiveness and safety claims.
- Export/API fields, device-source conflicts, duplicate handling and offline
  behavior.
- Accessibility, localization and manual-entry parity.
