# Activity Tier B — targeted evidence pass

Status: broad Tier B product pass complete; direct captures and science audit remain  
Updated: 2026-07-25

## Evidence boundary

This is a targeted scan of each product's distinctive contribution. It does
not imply equal depth, endorse vendor claims or define Body widget umbrellas.

## Strength programming and logging

### JEFIT

- Fast set/rep/load logging, prior-performance recall, timers and notes.
- Progress views cover volume, PRs, estimated 1RM and muscle-group frequency.
- Current Progressive Overload material describes separate strength, stimulus
  and movement-balance engines; only parts of that system are currently live.

### Boostcamp

- Joins program discovery, set-by-set execution and progression/history.
- Tracks RPE/RIR, volume, estimated 1RM, PRs and muscle-group engagement.
- Program structure and logged performance drive automatic progression.

### RP Hypertrophy

- Adaptive hypertrophy coaching focuses on volume progression, proximity to
  failure, fatigue management and deload timing.
- The critical evidence still missing is the exact live decision flow from
  session feedback to next-session sets, reps and load.

### TrainHeroic

- Relevant distinction is a coach-authored program delivered to an athlete,
  with training execution, performance assessment and coach communication.
- Current public first-party evidence is too thin for score or visual claims;
  direct app capture remains mandatory.

Product distinction: logging, following a program, generating progression and
coach/athlete delivery are related workflows but not interchangeable.

## Running plans

### Runna

- A goal, date, experience and schedule produce an adaptive running plan.
- Today joins the planned workout, expert context and weekly mileage.
- Plan management covers missed training, availability, pace/RPE targets,
  elevation, race estimates and device sync.
- Activity history includes planned and unplanned sessions.

Product distinction: planned work and completed activity need a visible
relationship without treating them as the same fact.

## Outdoor route products

### AllTrails

- Discovery, saved planning, live navigation/recording, activity history and
  profile/community are separate surfaces.
- During activity it prioritizes map position, distance, pace, elevation,
  remaining route and off-route safety.

### Komoot

- End-to-end flow is discover → plan → navigate → record → share/contribute.
- Route context includes elevation profile, surface, difficulty, waypoints and
  sport-specific paths.
- Personal heatmaps visualize where the user repeatedly travels.

Product distinction: route, navigation state and physiological activity are
different data products even when recorded in one session.

## Connected and guided training

### Zwift

- Weekly goals can use time, distance, calories, stress points or kilojoules.
- Training Score combines load volume/intensity/frequency.
- Training Status compares 42-day fitness with 7-day fatigue and maps the
  relation into readiness states.
- Indoor and supported outdoor rides can share the same fitness history.

### TrainerRoad

- Workout Levels rank relative workout difficulty per energy system from 1–10,
  using interval/recovery structure beyond TSS and IF.
- Athlete Levels represent current ability by training zone and change from
  completed-workout difficulty and success.
- The pairing of workout demand and athlete ability drives adaptation.

### Peloton

- Live class experience joins instruction with modality-specific metrics.
- Strive Score is a cross-modality, personal effort measure based on time in
  heart-rate zones; “Typical Strive” compares similar class type/duration.
- History, streaks, personal bests and milestones support adherence.

Product distinction: an exercise can be represented as prescribed demand,
live execution, completed performance, physiological load and long-term
adaptation. These must remain separate facts before later synthesis.

## Cross-product evidence dimensions

- plan versus completed session;
- technique/instruction versus measurement;
- external load versus internal load;
- acute session output versus longitudinal progression;
- route/navigation context versus physiology;
- individual training versus coach/program/community relationship.

These are research dimensions, **not** a proposed umbrella list.

## Remaining evidence tasks

- Direct current captures for all ten products.
- Stronger first-party reconstruction for TrainHeroic.
- Formula/version audit for every proprietary progression or load score.
- Sport-specific edge cases, offline behavior and manual entry fallbacks.
- Independent scientific review of training recommendations.
