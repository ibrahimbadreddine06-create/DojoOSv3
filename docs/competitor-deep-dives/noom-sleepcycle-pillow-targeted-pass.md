# Remaining Tier B targets — Noom, Sleep Cycle and Pillow

Status: targeted first-party pass complete  
Updated: 2026-07-25

## Evidence boundary

This closes the first broad Tier B product pass. It does not validate vendor
efficacy/accuracy claims or replace direct app capture.

## Noom

- Food logging is joined with a behavior-change curriculum rather than treated
  as an isolated diary.
- Its food color system primarily maps calorie density (calories divided by
  grams or milliliters) into green, yellow and orange guidance.
- The classification is directional rather than a moral good/bad label.
- Program surfaces connect intake awareness, calorie target, weight progress
  and lessons.

Distinctive lesson: a food fact, a classification and a behavior intervention
are three different layers. Body must preserve that separation.

## Sleep Cycle

- Phone microphone/accelerometer signals infer sleep patterns, sounds and
  stages; platform capabilities can differ.
- The 2026 Sleep Score uses Duration, Quality and Routine, with Routine
  deliberately foregrounded.
- Duration includes recent sleep debt; Quality covers continuity/stages,
  latency, efficiency and awake time; Routine covers regularity, timing and
  social jetlag.
- Smart Alarm uses an estimated lighter-sleep phase inside a wake window.
- Sleep notes support correlation views against historical sleep, not causal
  conclusions.

Distinctive lesson: observation method, score version and platform must be
stored with the result. A changed score model cannot silently overwrite the
meaning of historical scores.

## Pillow

- Pillow produces a 0–100 sleep score from total sleep, session duration,
  stages and awakenings/disruptions.
- Sleep rings provide compact quality and duration views.
- Phone/watch data, automatic session detection and widgets form distinct
  capture/display surfaces.

Distinctive lesson: a familiar ring is only valid when its denominator and
underlying source are explicit.

## Remaining evidence tasks

- Direct current capture across free/paid tiers and supported platforms.
- Independent audit of sleep-stage and score validation.
- Formula versioning and missing-data behavior.
- Scientific/clinical validation of Noom claims beyond product architecture.
