# Widget umbrella specification — Sleep Efficiency

Status: definitive method-labelled contract  
Umbrella ID: `rest.sleep_efficiency`

Question: **What share of the eligible in-bed interval was scored as sleep?**

Essential `1×1`: percentage, night/period, method/source and validity.

Body calculation: eligible sleep duration divided by eligible time in bed
under a versioned boundary rule. Provider values stay provider-owned if their
method cannot be reproduced.

Variants: value/trend, night composition. Initial sizes: `1×1`, `1×2`, `2×1`,
`2×2`. Efficiency alone is not sleep quality.
