# Widget umbrella specification — Routine Consistency

Status: definitive calculated contract  
Umbrella ID: `hygiene.routine_consistency`

Question: **How consistently did I complete the selected routine against my
own schedule?**

Essential `1×1`: eligible completion result, period, routine and coverage.

Calculation uses completed eligible opportunities divided by scheduled
eligible opportunities under versioned timezone/missed-day rules. Schedule
changes retain history.

Variants: consistency value, calendar/field, trend. Initial sizes:
`1×1`, `1×2`, `2×1`, `2×2`. No “habit formed” date.
