# Body competitor research completion ledger

Status: active research gate  
Updated: 2026-07-25

## Why this ledger exists

The first product synthesis started after only nine deep dives and produced an
invalid grouping layer above real widget umbrellas. This ledger prevents
research planning from being mistaken for completed research.

No umbrella selection may restart until:

1. every Tier A product has a source-audited reconstruction;
2. every Tier B product has a targeted pass for its distinctive capability;
3. every Tier C product has a gap scan or documented reason to defer;
4. the passes have been synthesized per Body submodule;
5. calculations, visible products and visual patterns remain separate evidence
   dimensions;
6. the correct hierarchy is used:
   Body submodule → widget umbrella → widget variant → supported size.

## What “official evidence pass” means

An official evidence pass records the product architecture, visible
information, disclosed calculations, visual/interaction grammar, limitations
and remaining unknowns from first-party material. It is not yet equivalent to:

- direct in-app capture on every platform/tier/region;
- independent scientific validation;
- permission to copy a proprietary calculation;
- proof of an accessible API;
- a Body widget or umbrella decision.

## Preliminary audit of the original nine files

All nine original files contain:

- an explicit evidence boundary;
- product architecture or information structure;
- calculations/formulas where available;
- visual grammar;
- strengths/limitations;
- open evidence tasks.

They range from roughly 134 to 400 lines and collectively reference official
sources. That structural completeness does **not** prove statement-level source
accuracy. Before synthesis, each still requires:

1. every material claim linked to supporting evidence;
2. stale statements checked against the current product;
3. calculation inputs/windows/formula versions verified;
4. device, subscription, country and age gates recorded;
5. direct visual capture where public documentation is insufficient;
6. “unknown” retained wherever evidence does not close the question.

## Per-submodule synthesis

The first pre-umbrella synthesis is recorded in
`body-submodule-evidence-synthesis.md`. It separates observable data, derived
interpretation, user jobs, time model, provenance, visualization, interaction,
missingness and risk. None of those evidence dimensions is treated as an
umbrella.
